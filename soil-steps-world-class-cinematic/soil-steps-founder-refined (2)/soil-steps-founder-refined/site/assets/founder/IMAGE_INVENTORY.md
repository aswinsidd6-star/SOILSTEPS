## Notes on completeness

- **All 33 pages were reviewed.** Every distinct photograph, certificate, medal, and
  trophy image is either displayed on the Founder page or listed above with the
  reason it wasn't (near-duplicate sequence frame, or a plain text/announcement
  page with no photographic content).
- **Near-duplicate frames not displayed** (page03_1, page04_1, page13_2, page31_2)
  are burst-sequence shots of a moment already shown from a stronger frame. They
  remain in the project's source folder (`/home/claude/work/cropped2/` at build
  time) and can be added to the gallery on request.
- **No image was unreadable or failed to extract** — all 33 pages rendered cleanly
  at source resolution (904×1279 px per page).
- **No certificate was cropped, recoloured, or altered.** All are shown at their
  original proportions and can be opened full-size in the lightbox.
- Captions throughout use only names, dates, and titles that appear verbatim in
  the certificates, the booklet's bio page, or the newspaper clipping — nothing
  was inferred beyond what two independent sources (e.g. photo + certificate)
  confirmed. Where a face was recognisable but not independently confirmable
  (e.g. background portraits at the Chennai Secretariat), no name was assigned.
